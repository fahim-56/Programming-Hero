CSS Positions

  1. Static
  2. Relative (Changes relative to its current positions. Don't lose its own position)
  3. Absolute(Changes relative to its absolute positions/from the begging of page/ parent position. Loose its own positions.)
  4. Fixed(Fixed into a common position of screen.Loose its own positions)
  5. Sticky(Fixed into a common position of screen until it comes to its own position.Doesn't loose its own positions)
